update: in college(?!)

2022-10-10

 hey, so it's been a minute. I'd like to update those reading that I am now a student at the University of California, Berkeley. Life's gone on, and this blog remains the may pole that doesn't move. I'm going to upload photos of life here, I want to be able to track everything on this. I'll try and keep this more updated. 

So far this has been really stressful. I'm having a bit of an identity crisis right now. I keep mirroring people (term I learned yesterday), but there are so many people here that I don't know who to cling on to. I become a confused, misdirected mess, and unhappy with myself
