complete piq's (#2)

2021-11-21








Prompt: Think of an academic subject that inspires you. Describe how you’ve furthered this interest inside and/or outside of the classroom.


Word Count Minimum/Maximum: 350


While in the heart of the pandemic, I searched for something that would make my experience a little more productive. I had always been an avid reader, and with all the chaos occurring around me, I started reading more about politics, public health, and society in publications such as The New York Times and The Washington Post. I realized the importance of the truth and began to have a deeper respect for open discourse regarding issues of the day.


My high school didn’t have its own publication, but other public high schools in San Francisco did. My friend and I proposed starting one to the principal, and she gave us permission to do so.


Although I enjoyed writing a few pieces on my own, self-expression was not the only thing that I got out of this club. As the co-editor, I was able to give other students a voice. My creation gave me and others a medium to express ourselves. And because of this, I learned to listen to other people’s opinions. I learned to respect them, and to find common ground.


Running The Balboa Buc, our school’s student publication, I wet my feet in the world of journalism. It gave me a sense of the possibilities that I could pursue in life. I know that most colleges have a school newspaper, and eager to continue my passion from high school, I’m excited to join one.


It’s not every day that you find something you love to do that also offers career paths. It’s not every day that you find something that you enjoy doing that also helps you grow in the process. Founding my school’s newspaper has given me a great deal. It’s taught me how to listen to others’ opinions. It’s taught me how to think critically, and it’s helped me find my voice, both in my writing and in my life.
