little words that catch my head

2023-12-10

 Dear Blog, 

I'm writing you at a point of potential need. Let me explain. Consistently during a period of heightened stress, (finals would be that period for me currently), I come to a wall in my path. I speed through a lot of life, never really stopping to see what's around me and understand where I'm at. But there comes time that I am forced to confront my current position, and unable to go further. At these point, points where I've become so "post-stressed," I zoom way out and start analyzing the world around me from a (to be honest) self centered point of view. I consider everyone's actions as a reaction to me, rather than considering them to be a reaction of themselves. I stress over everyone's little words, and their little actions. I become hyper critical, and fearful for the notes that I have missed at the regular speed of my life. It becomes hard to breathe, to think and to understand. I'd liken it to a long term panic attack, one that can't be thought out of, slept out of, or drank out of. I guess I just have to let it slide. I think that the change in sun-time has been a contributing factor. I just lose my sense of self, and lose the sense of self that I share with others. I start panicking over my relationships with friends. More recently, I failed at a joke that in retrospect dabbled in misogyny, and am deeply remorseful for it. I think that my reaction is overblown, and just heightened by my already stressed state of being. 

I'm not really sure what the answer to this is. I'm not excited to go on break, I'm not excited for the semester to end. I guess this is what I was talking about when I said that there is no way to think my way out of this. Normally, I can just look forward to a better time, but with winter break on the cusp, I don't have anything to look forward to. I think I might just be seasonally depressed. I think that I can't see what's exciting because of that. I'm a little hopeless but I don't have the energy to pull myself out of that. I'll gain it back somehow and sometime, so I'm not that deep in the ruts. Idk, times heals all. 

I'm on track to finish everything on time. I'll get through this. Hopefully everyone else is just stressed and not disdainful for me. I bet that's what's happening.

esm
