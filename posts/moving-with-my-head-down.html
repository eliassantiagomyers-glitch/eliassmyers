moving with my head down

2024-11-02

Dear Blog, 

Tonight, I drank two beers and went to one of the worst gatherings of people I’ve been to in college. I came home not only feeling upset but profoundly sad. I thought I’d pretense that before you read what I wrote when I got home. I’ll probably try and decrypt it later, with the hopes that I unwillingly said something useful to myself. Or at the very minimum, non-consequential. 


Yesterday was Halloween. I went as Abel, Luca as Cain. We walked around with our copies of Genesis. Today was an essay day. Tomorrow will be leaps and bounds better than today. I'm getting coffee with Matthew at 10 in the morning to look over the ballot and decide on propositions.

--

I thought earlier about the speed of time, and the momentum of my experiences and memories.

I thought earlier that I had to low them down. I thought I was whistling through with my head down and my eyes in the back of my head. 

Chaitan said something a few weeks ago while in a screaming match in the Kingman dining room. He said that the truth was not in the object itself, but rather in the description of the object. 

That it isn’t the material object of accomplishment, the completion of a task, or the experience of contentment that hold the form I’m seeking. Rather, it’s different points that I’ve touched trying to land myself there. The beauty is the different interpretations of my losses and my victories (which fairly can’t be described as losses or victories if we accept that they are all just the definition). This thought in writing seems more or less straightforward, but I’ve been thinking about that a lot recently.

I’ve been overwhelmed this week. Although it was mostly classwork that got me to this point, stress finds a way to infect all that it touches. 

And during this time, I’ve been thinking a lot about movement. Am I blowing through things and crashing when time stops? I wrote earlier as a joke that “the days sucks and the nights are boring.” I’ll change that: 

The days are long, and the night are longer. But the days are also short, and the nights, shorter. 

I can’t simply wait to get there. I don’t need to slow down or speed up. I just need to look up.
