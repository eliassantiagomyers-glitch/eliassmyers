2023-12-06


dear blog,

You've caught me in my typical space. I'm trying not to work on a paper about how the trial of Dmitri Karamazov is in conversation with the story of Cain and Abel in Genesis. I don't know. Here's the highlights: “Who is responsible for his life?” - Dostoevsky. And, "Am I my brother's keeper?" - God.

I'm sitting on the floor in Becks room. He's playing an electronic sound over and over again that I think is a song but I'm not sure. 

I started this draft a few hours ago, while in the library. I've left since then, played with the band, ate stove orzo, and got here. Where have you been?
