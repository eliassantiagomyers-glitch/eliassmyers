first sleep in barcelona

2025-01-10

It’s early. The sky is getting lighter above the courtyard, but the sun remains down. The pigeons on my window are starting to wake up, but I’m still sitting in the dark in my hut. 
Last night I hung out with my host and her friend. She was a jeweler and he was a puppet designer. We drank beer and talked. He kept calling me “chino,” and when I told him I’m not, he kept calling me “indio.” He kept giving me fist bumps. 

I slept walked. I had a dream of two lines, one still and one vibrating. The vibrating line terrified me. I can’t explain the fear. I was afraid that the vibrations couldn’t be controlled and was lost in chaos. I’ve slept walked before. But this time, it was different. This was lucid. I was awake and sleep, in control and helpless. I knew I was moving. I consciously went to the bathroom to shake myself out of the nightmare, and consciously returned to my hut. I was cautious not to wake anyone, much less talk to anyone. But I was asleep. I was dreaming, and I couldn't pinch my way out of it. 

I think it was indigestion. My stomach's not used to Spain.
