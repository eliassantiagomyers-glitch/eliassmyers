complete piq's (#1)

2021-11-21








Prompt: Describe how you have taken advantage of a significant educational opportunity or worked to overcome an educational barrier you have faced.


Word Count Minimum/Maximum:


While the world shut down, while school shut down, while every single aspect of my life shut down, I looked for opportunities. Facing the prospect of a year online, I yearned to make the best of my situation.


Balboa High School doesn’t offer many AP classes, but I had taken AP World History my sophomore year and really liked it.


“Why don’t I study on my own for an AP test that Balboa doesn’t offer?”, I thought to myself.


Restrictive as distance-learning was, it did give me time. Time to focus on school, time to find new interests, and time to reflect on who I was and who I wanted to be. I took distance-learning as an opportunity to teach myself AP US History, both because of my love for the topic and to make use of the extra time I had on my hands.


I purchased a study book and dedicated an hour every day to studying. I had never been someone with strong willpower; I often procrastinate, and let myself off the hook. But I saw this as the time to change. I was driven to prove to myself that I didn’t need an instructor; that I had control enough to monitor myself to prepare for the test.


Over the course of a year, I saw my study habits change. I was less tempted to leave something for tomorrow. I knew that if I didn't hold myself accountable, nobody would. I taught myself how to manage time and balance the responsibilities in my life.


By the end of the year, after all my methodical hard work, I was as ready to take the test as anyone else who had taken the actual course. Although I had no instructor, classmates, or textbook, I was ready. It was a surprise to me to find out that I had received a 5. But over the year, I learned more than what I needed to earn a 5. While studying for this test, I learned how to manage my time. I learned how to hold myself accountable, and I learned self-responsibility.
