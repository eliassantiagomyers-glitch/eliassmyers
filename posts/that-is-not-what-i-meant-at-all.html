That is not what I meant, at all

2025-03-10

Oh, damn sky! 
Can I get a break from this rain! 
Can I have but a glimpse of the blue sea above? 
 
No, for that would be fair, and life’s not fair. 
    You know that. 
It would be unfair to all the wet bodies around 
    to let you walk on dry ground. 
It would be unfair for the trail to be clear, 
    with nil but small bushes lonely on the hill. 
It would be unfair for words like Yes and This Way? to be posed with intent. 
Life exists in the shadows (maybe gray with clouds, maybe full of rain, sagging, and leaking), and in the light, it is all but true. 
 
In the light you see what you shouldn’t, couldn’t, mustn’t. 
 
Keeps the crowd laughing, with minds on the cliff of the next joke, 
    leaning, 
        leaning—
 
But not falling. 
Held back by the fear of the ground, 
    the fear of the fall, but mostly the fear of having already fallen—
        nowhere else to fall, 
            no heights left to fear. 
 
Oh sky, why must it rain? 
 
I can hear the laughter, oh how they laugh. 
    Through the snickers of pouring tears, life sprouts, grows, dies. 
 
How they laugh—
    louder than the ticking copper watch on my right wrist, turning blue with poisonous oxygen—

Bit, by the time it fruitlessly creates
