crossing the border

2025-01-08

I think they call it the “yoop”—the part of Michigan that juts out from the northeast corner of Wisconsin. That’s where I am right now. In theory, I should be able to see Lake Superior out my window. I’m sitting on the far-left side, next to the window. It’s just me in my row.  
Under me is a thick, textured blanket of clouds. When I woke up from my nap, I thought I was looking at the heaviest snowpack I’ve seen, but it’s not snow. It’s just clouds. There’s a little GPS on the screen in front of me. It’s hard to see exactly where I am, but I think I’ll be crossing the American-Canadian border in the next 20 minutes. 
I cut myself shaving this morning. I couldn’t sleep—my mind kept racing with what-ifs and hypotheticals about things that could go wrong. I mistyped my name on my ticket. It says “Eliass,” and I couldn’t sleep with the thought that they’d turn me away for only being “Elias.” When I got up to shower, my body was near vegetative. It was 4:45 AM and I hadn’t slept. I half joked that I was leaving a little bit of myself behind. 
On my way out, I bought a cup of Peet’s at the stand in the airport. I’m savoring it. It’s cold now. I know there’s coffee in Europe, and good coffee at that, but I tried the “Lavazza” coffee Air Canada brews, and I realized that I might be in for a long haul. Supposedly Lavazza is common in Europe. 
I’m about 5 minutes away from the border now. 
I’ll post this when I land in Montreal. 
au revoir
elias
