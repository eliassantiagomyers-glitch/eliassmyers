1/2 through summer

2024-07-06

dear blog

It's the beginning of July, and summer has found its legs. I've been on the road, and have found my time gridlocked with activity. Being an unusually busy summer, I have regrettably not been communicating as much as people have expected me to. I was actually feeling like my recent silence has been a vacation from the social stress that does not dissipate with the conclusion of finals. Here I am, sitting at the kitchen table, writing this blog with a minor hope that posting this will awaken my desire to walk back into the social light, and beg forgiveness to all those I've turned from. There's an email waiting to be written to Georgia, and a couple dozen apology texts to Ella, along with various employers. I've been moving recently. A weekend in Morro Bay, a week in LA, three weeks at a summer camp in the redwoods---there hasn't been a down period for the last month. Summer started slow: A few days in the north coast, followed by two weeks of extreme boredom. I quickly realized that if I wanted this to be something, I needed to be advantageous and aggressive. Maybe I was too aggressive. Maybe I'm tired. Yesterday was the Fourth of July, and I spent the day looking for swimming holes in the Napa valley with Bella. Monday I'm headed up to Mendocino National Forest to feed on the feeling of non responsibility and isolation. I don't need more vacation time, but I'm chasing the feeling of a never ending trip, in which coming home is the liminal time, the space between traveling. I'll be back in Los Angeles for annual session in the coming weeks. Supposedly I'm going camping in Santa Cruz the weekend before that though...when is that? Next Friday? I can't keep track of the days, but that sounds right. I might be going to Sacramento the day after tomorrow, the day before leaving for Mendocino. I've been toying with the idea of buying one of the super cheap airplane tickets, from Frontier or Spirit or something, and just getting myself to the east coast. Not that there's much for me out there, just a car and the farm. I'd probably head up to New York City, but that might cost more trouble than it's worth. Plus, how many times can I visit the Seinfeld dinner before they ask me to buy something? 

If any of that happens, I'll be sure you're the first to know,

With love from my corner of the world, 

esmyers
