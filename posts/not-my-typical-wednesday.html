Not my typical Wednesday

2023-11-23


Dear Blog,

Today was the first day of break, and although it's nice to return home, it's also harsh. Going to bed at 10, waking up fully rested with nothing to do is a jump away from my last four months in Berkeley. I fixed my bike today. The rear wheel had been punctured (figured that one out when I pulled everything apart), and the tube needed replacing. Took a bit of elbow grease--the tire was resistant to being removed. 

After that, I visited a few second-hand stores and bought a few different things. I'll attach some photos below. There's these really neat small clay cups, hand painted and cast and all. A nice shirt, and a nice sweater. A felt rollable curtain that I haggled down to two and a half bucks. 

I'm sitting on the couch reading Tintin and listening to the Charlie Brown Christmas album. So far so good.
