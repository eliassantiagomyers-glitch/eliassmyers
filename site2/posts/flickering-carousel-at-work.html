flickering carousel at work

2024-09-04

Dear blog, 

the light on this study carousel is blinking slightly, but I'm so caffeinated and focused that I don't really mind. Typically, that would bother me. So would the way that my screen protector isn't fully adhered and bubbled. Or how my watch is slightly loose, or how my waistband is sweaty. I typically would be annoyed by the stubble on my face, due to my dull razor. Typically these things would bother me, but recently they haven't. They've just been flies in the kitchen--annoying, yes, but understandable, manageable, and ignorable. 

I don't know how long this will last. Maybe I'll get irritated by the smoothness of it all.
