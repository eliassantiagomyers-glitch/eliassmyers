A rekindling of an old, long lost friend.

2023-11-21

 Dear Blog, 

It has truly been a minute. Since the last post, I've been around and about---experiencing, tasting, and smelling new things. I've eaten a lot of good food and told a lot of bad jokes. I've been good! How have you been? I saw that everything got reverted to "draft" for about 6 months. What was that about?! Well, anyways, I'll hit you with the murky and the mundane in a bit. I want to become a more active contributor to this site, I think the value this provides to us all completely outweighs the work of keeping it active. 

I truly missed you, and hope that with the light of the new year radiating over the threshold of time ("ew" -elias) we will grow our life-vines back together as a singular stem. ("even worse" -Elias)

till then!

I leave you with a portion---the final verse---of Robert Frost's The Road Not Taken, a personal favorite. 

And so it goes:

I shall be telling this with a sigh
Somewhere ages and ages hence:
Two roads diverged in a wood, and I—
I took the one less traveled by,
And that has made all the difference.
