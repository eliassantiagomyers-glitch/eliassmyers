back in berkeley, and thoughts on the new levels of blog readers

2023-11-27

 Dear Blog, 

I'm back in the east bay now, attempting to finish the last three weeks of the semester. It's weird being in this city, but it's also weird coming back. Nothing about this place is actually real. I'm excited to be living a life that is not dependent on me paying tuition. 

It's currently freezing. That is to say it's a low 50. 

I've noticed that readership of this blog has tripled over the last week. It must be word of mouth. I don't imagine my instagram gets a lot of visits from people outside of my close circle, and given that they haven't told me anything about reading this, I'm not sure who it can be. (if you read this, comment!!!) Perhaps it's just me looking at the page myself in different tabs. I agree, it's great that readership is up, but naturally, as readership goes up, privacy is inverted. Perhaps I'll open a more private page for those purposes, but it's not like I'm that exposed here. 

till later, 

e. myers
