Late August, Early September

2024-09-03




Dear blog

It’s become almost a tradition that I return to this site during times of movement. I’ve been driving a lot, so I guess it’s time to scream into the void. Welcome back world.

The house has worn new clothes in the last few weeks. A place that’s divided me for so long has somehow come to reunite my love for the space and the people. There were a few tight turns at home with my dad’s health, but the road seems clear from here. Overall, it feels like this highway I’m on for the next few months is mine to create---nothing has been determined.

Also it feels like I’ve overdone the car metaphor. 

Well, regardless, the point gets across. I’ve had a good summer—a classic, American summer of traveling and driving and swimming and building—and I’m prepared for a good fall. I’m prepared to stress and unravel, to procrastinate and dissolve. I’m prepared to get bitter and uncreative. But I’m also ready to grab these remaining months in Berkeley by the handle and really do something. 

Or maybe I’ll just buy a car.
