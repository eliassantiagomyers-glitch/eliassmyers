summer reading

2024-07-12

I often think about the books that I've started and never finished. I think about the chapters that were left mid-conversation, and wonder where that story was taking me. Sometimes I return to those books. Sometimes, the memories of inside jokes and shared memories pour back into like water from a boiling kettle, dripping over coffee grounds, bringing that caffeine of life back into my slowing cycles. I often think about the people and memories I was prescribed even though I was not yet smart enough to take the dose. I think about the times when good things happen to me and how I run from the moment just to bask in the joy and safety of the memory. I think about what things could have been like if I were to just commit, just jump, just read to the end. But I don't. I find new books and books I've already read before and my phone and all kinds of distractions that keep me from flipping the page I'm on and being transformed by the journey I'm in. 

I wonder if these books can still be picked up. Or maybe it's been too long and I've forgotten the plots and characters. Maybe they've forgotten me. Maybe I've forgotten how to read these books. I bet it's too late to pick a few of them up. Thoughts like these have me looking for a library, eager to replace the lost book with a new book to get lost in. I tell myself that it isn't a habit, it's just poor luck. I tell myself that I just haven't found a book I like, or that I'm just not ready to read a new book, not ready to love a book I will eventually put down. I feel guilty for all the past books, and seek my future in the unfinished teachings of all the stories I haven't read.
