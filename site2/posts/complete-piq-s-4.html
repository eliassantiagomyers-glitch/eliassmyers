complete piq's (#4)

2021-11-21








Prompt: What have you done to make your school or your community a better place?


Word Count Minimum/Maximum:


Amongst the people at my school, I started to notice a problem. People all around me were getting hooked on JUULs and other vape products. The problem got real for me when close friends started getting addicted, altering entire relationships. The vaping epidemic affected a lot of people, and it had finally reached me.


While trying to find what I could do to address this problem, I learned of an opportunity run out of Stanford University; a Youth Advisory Board to help Stanford’s teen tobacco prevention program develop an ‘alternative to vaping’ curriculum.


Through the Stanford Youth Advisory Board, I was able to meet other teens with a passion for this issue. The other youth that I met through this program had incredible stories and experiences. Because of them, I was inspired to do more, and want more out of the world.


We brainstormed different ways that we could effectively confront the issue of teen tobacco abuse. We ran webinars (with upwards of 300 attendees), where we gave our own personal experience to parents, teachers, and youth. We wrote letters to our state representatives in support of the California Senate Bill 793, which implemented stronger flavored tobacco regulations, ultimately culminating in banning the sale of flavored tobacco products in California.


The thing I’m most proud of is that through my work, I may have made a difference in someone’s life. I hope that I may have been the reason that someone quit vaping, and sought help. Although vaping is still prevalent, I’ve been able to see the effects that I had on this problem.
