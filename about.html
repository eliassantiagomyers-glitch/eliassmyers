About

I'm a journalist and writer from San Francisco, California. I graduated from UC Berkeley in May 2026 with a degree in History and Public Policy, concentrating in late twentieth-century Californian industrial history.

I'm currently reporting for ChicoSol, a non-profit newsroom covering Northern California. At ChicoSol, I've been covering law enforcement, immigration, and surveillance. My work can be found here.

At Berkeley, I served four years on the Berkeley Political Review, where, as Editor-in-Chief, I significantly grew the publication's daily readership and managed a large editorial staff.

My reporting and writing focuses on politics, labor, and California — particularly the economic history of the Bay Area, deindustrialization, and the growing elusivity of the American Dream.
